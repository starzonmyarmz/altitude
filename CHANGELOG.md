### Added

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added
- Altitude, stacked octaves for guitar, bass, synths or anything else, as a VST3 and CLAP plugin for macOS and Windows.
- Five floors to mix: two octaves down (Cave), one down (Valley), your dry signal (You), one up (Ridge) and two up (Peak), each with a switch light to bring it in and out.
- Tone, Attack and Detune knobs, and a footswitch.
- Eight presets, from a bass under guitar to a swell pad, stepped through in a window on the pedal.
